Water Pumps Partner

DAB Pumps S.p.A.

Founded in 1975 and headquartered in Mestrino, near Padova, Italy, DAB Pumps has spent over 50 years developing pumping technology for residential, commercial and industrial water management. The company is known for energy-efficient circulators, submersible and borehole pumps, booster and pressurisation systems, wastewater and drainage pumps, and smart electronic ranges such as the EsyBox Line — all designed around water and energy efficiency.

Fire Pumps Partner

Eifel Pump (Fuzhou) Corp. Ltd

Eifel Pump is a China-based manufacturer specialising in industrial, commercial and fire-protection pumping systems. Its UL Listed fire pump ranges — end suction and double-suction split-case designs, plus jockey pumps — are built to UL 448 standards and suited to NFPA 20 fire protection systems. Eifel holds ISO 9001:2015 quality management certification, and selected models are approved by Sri Lanka's Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) Fire Department for buildings up to 30 m in height.

UL Listed & ISO 9001:2015

Fire pumps certified to UL 448, suitable for NFPA 20 systems, from an ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer.

CMC-approved for Sri Lanka

Selected fire pump models approved by the Colombo Municipal Council Fire Department for buildings up to 30 m.

Complete fire pump systems

UL Listed end suction (EHF) and split-case (ESF) fire pumps, and FDL jockey pumps for sprinkler mains.
