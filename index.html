About Aquarise

Sri Lanka's trusted partner for water & fire pump systems

Aquarise Agencies (Pvt) Ltd is a leading provider of pumping and fluid handling solutions in Sri Lanka, specialising in the supply and maintenance of high-performance pumping systems for commercial, industrial and infrastructure applications. As the sole authorized representative for EIFEL and DAB brands in Sri Lanka, we provide genuine products, technical expertise, installation support and after-sales services.

Experienced engineering team

Qualified engineers and trained technicians with proven project execution capability.

Technical expertise

System design, pump selection and performance optimisation across water supply, fire protection, boosting, wastewater and industrial applications.

Fast response, genuine parts

Prompt support from our Gampaha workshop, with original spare parts for long-term reliability.

After Sales Service & Maintenance

Comprehensive customer support

Aquarise provides complete lifecycle support for all supplied systems — from installation to long-term maintenance.

Installation Supervision

Professional fitting and installation oversight of pump systems by qualified technicians.

Commissioning & Testing

Full system testing and commissioning to confirm performance before handover.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance programs and emergency breakdown support to keep systems running.

Spare Parts & Consultation

Genuine spare parts, performance evaluations and technical consultations on request.
